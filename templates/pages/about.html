About Islington Marketplace

The campus marketplace for Islington College students

What We Do

Islington Marketplace is a dedicated online platform designed exclusively for the students and community of Islington College. We provide a safe, convenient, and trusted environment where students can buy and sell products directly within the college premises.

Our Mission

Our mission is to create a thriving campus marketplace that connects students, making it easy to find great deals on textbooks, electronics, clothing, stationery, and much more. By keeping transactions within the college, we ensure safety, convenience, and community building.

How It Works

  • Register — Create your free account in seconds.
  • Browse — Explore products from fellow students.
  • Connect — Message sellers directly to negotiate.
  • Meet — Arrange a meetup at one of our 4 campus locations.
  • Review — Leave a review to help other students.

Campus Locations

All meetups happen safely within Islington College premises at these designated spots:

Kumari Hall

One of the main gathering spots on campus. Ideal for meetups and product exchanges.

BRIT Cafe

The campus cafe — a relaxed spot for buyers and sellers to meet over a cup of tea.

Main Block

The central academic building — a convenient and well-known meeting point.

Skill Block

Perfect for tech and electronics meetups in the skills training area.
